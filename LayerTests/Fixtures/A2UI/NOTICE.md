# A2UI examples

The `a2ui-*.json` files here are official A2UI 1.0 examples from
[a2ui-project/a2ui](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui) at commit `07735d6`
(`07735d6a654ae3ba25951a8753ddd57b344b1169`, tag `v0.11.0+swift`), copied from
`catalogs/basic/v1/examples/`. They are unmodified apart from their names, which gained an
`a2ui-` prefix because a test bundle's resources share one folder.
